Short video script, 45–60 sec

Title: Before You Build the App

[0–5s — Visual: founder looking at a Figma mockup / AI app builder / landing page]
Voiceover:
You have an app idea.
A product.
A workflow.
Maybe even a prototype.

[5–12s — Visual: login, database, payments, dashboard icons appearing]
And the next question feels simple:
“How do I build this?”

[12–22s — Visual: icons start stacking into a heavy backend diagram]
But the moment your app adds users, login, payments, profiles, support access, or stored data, you are not just building features anymore.

You are creating responsibility.

[22–34s — Visual: database/admin panel/access rules/security warning]
Someone has to decide who can access the data.
Where it lives.
Who can recover it.
Who can delete it.
Who can support users without seeing too much.

That “simple backend” becomes the place where authority accumulates.

[34–46s — Visual: split screen: traditional app-owned backend vs user-owned state with app interacting safely]
Sovereignbase starts from a different question:

Before your app owns all user data by default, should it?

[46–56s — Visual: app UI stays clean; backend burden fades into structured architecture]
Sovereignbase helps developers build real applications without becoming the legal, operational, and technical authority over user data.

[56–60s — Visual: logo / CTA]
Build the app.
Don’t become the data authority.
Sovereignbase.
