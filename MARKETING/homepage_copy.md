Your application needs a backend. It needs a way to store data, identify users, collect payments, keep state in sync, support offline use, react to changes, and connect the user interface to the data behind it.

Most backends solve this by moving your users’ data into your custody. Your application becomes the place where their data lives and dies. That makes development familiar, but it also makes you responsible for securing, managing, and operating it.

You should not have to take on more data responsibility than your product requires.

With Sovereignbase, you get a user-sovereign Backend-as-a-Service that gives you the backend capabilities you need while keeping user data controlled from the user’s side.

Most of the state logic runs on the user’s device. Your app works with user-owned resources directly. As a developer, you mount those resources into UI components, listen to their state, and update them from the interface. Sovereignbase keeps them synchronized and backed up with end-to-end encryption, eliminating the need for a traditional app-owned database at the center.

This gives you a cleaner development model. The backend service provides infrastructure. The user’s device manages state. Your frontend reacts to that state. Your application can behave like a modern web app without centralizing user data on your server.

You reduce backend burden and get to focus on your app’s brand, and product experience.

Your users get a better default. Their data can stay under their control instead of being absorbed into another centralized backend.
